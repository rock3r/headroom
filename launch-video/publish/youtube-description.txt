Headroom: your AI limits, at a glance.

Headroom is a free, open source Android app that shows how much of your AI subscription limits you have left: Claude, ChatGPT Codex, GitHub Copilot, Grok, Z.AI, Kimi Code, OpenCode Go and JetBrains AI, all on one screen.

• Usage rate and forecasts: a tick on every bar shows an even pace. A wavy bar means you are using your quota faster than an even pace or have used more than 85%.
• See it coming: the detail view tells you when you'll hit the limit at your current rate.
• Use a reset in one tap: Codex and Grok resets refill your bars from a bottom sheet.
• Know the moment it resets: an alert, or the reset island that grows out of the camera cutout.
• Widgets for your home and lock screen: Rings, Bars, Shape and Countdown.
• Make it yours: eight palettes or your wallpaper's colours, light or dark.
