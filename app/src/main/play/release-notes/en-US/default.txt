Headroom 1.4.1 removes the sign-in notification 1.4.0 added, and the permission that came with it.

• No foreground service and no notification while you sign in to ZCode or Cursor. Come back to Headroom when you are done and it finishes the sign-in.
• Everything from 1.4.0 stays: Cursor usage for a Grok account, a ZCode sign-in that opens at once and shows your resets, and clearer messages when a sign-in fails.
