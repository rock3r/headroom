The first public release of Headroom: see how much of your AI subscription limits you have left,
on one screen and in widgets.

## What is in it

- **Eight providers.** Claude, ChatGPT Codex, GitHub Copilot, Grok, Z.AI, Kimi Code, OpenCode Go
  and JetBrains AI, with more than one account per provider if you have them.
- **Overview.** Each account shows its weekly or monthly usage, its session usage, and whether you
  are over or under an even pace. Sort the accounts, or drag them into your own order.
- **Reset alerts.** A heads-up notification when a weekly limit resets, checked against the
  provider so it only arrives for a real reset.
- **Resets and Stats.** The upcoming resets with their alert bells, the history of each window,
  and stats about how you use your quotas.
- **Widgets.** Rings, Bars, Shape and Countdown, for the home screen and the lock screen.
- **Settings.** Used or left, light or dark, your wallpaper's colours or one of eight palettes,
  reduced motion, and how often to update in the background.
- **Delights.** A sparkling sheen when new numbers arrive, and confetti when a limit resets while
  the app is open.
- **Reset island (experimental).** A small pill that grows out of the camera cutout when a limit
  resets while Headroom is in the background.

## Installing

Download `headroom-1.0.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. Headroom needs Android 16 or later.
You can check the download against `headroom-1.0.0.apk.sha256`.

If you built Headroom yourself before, uninstall that build first: it is signed with a different
key, so Android will not install this release over it.

Headroom reads usage from undocumented endpoints, and any provider can change them. It is not
affiliated with any of the providers.
