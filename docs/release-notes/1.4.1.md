Deepseek fucked up.

1.4.0 shipped a foreground service and an ongoing notification for the ZCode and Cursor sign-ins. That was a bad fix, and Play refused the 1.4.0 upload over it. 1.4.1 takes it out: no service, no notification, no permission, no Play declaration.

## What changed

- Headroom no longer runs a foreground service, and no longer posts a notification while a sign-in waits.
  The `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC` permissions are gone with it.
- Signing in to ZCode or Cursor from the browser still works: the page opens in Headroom's own task, so the tab is gone when you come back, and Headroom finishes the sign-in as soon as you return to it. The waiting line now says to come back.
- Everything else in 1.4.0 is unchanged: Cursor usage for a Grok account, the ZCode sheet that opens at once and stays open with the resets, the failed-sign-in messages, and the reset that is no longer counted twice in Stats.

## Why the notification was never needed

ZCode and Cursor finish their sign-ins on their own servers, and both pin the redirect to their own callback, so no page ever navigates back into Headroom the way Claude's, Codex's, JetBrains's and Grok's do. Headroom polls for the result instead.

The previous version kept the app awake so that poll could keep running while you were in the browser. That bought one thing: the app returned to the front by itself. It cost a permission, a permanent notification, and the Play upload. Coming back to Headroom yourself does the same job.

## Installing

Download `headroom-1.4.1.apk` from this release and open it on your phone.
Headroom requires Android 16 or later. Use `headroom-1.4.1.apk.sha256` to check the download.
This release updates 1.4.0 and preserves your accounts, their usage history and their reset records.
Obtainium can also install and update Headroom. The README contains its setup link.
