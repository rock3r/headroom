Headroom 1.1 lists available usage-limit resets and supports Codex and Grok redemption.
It also adds a Quick Settings tile.

## What is new

- Provider-issued resets refill limits early. Account details and the Resets tab list each account's resets and their expiry dates.
  Codex and Grok redemption opens a confirmation sheet that identifies affected limits. The bars refill after success.
  Claude resets remain read-only. This release does not display Z.AI resets.
- Add the Quick Settings tile from Settings. It opens Headroom and shows the next reset or the quota nearest its limit.
- Expired sign-ins keep their last values on screen with faded styling and a sign-in button.
  Headroom posts one warning notification. Signing in again preserves account history, name and alerts.
- Claude's one-time credits show usage and expiry dates, including dollar amounts when the provider reports them.
  Headroom displays unfamiliar quotas with an explanatory note. It also displays every model-specific weekly limit reported by Claude.
- Refresh shimmer and sparkles use soft rainbow colours. After a reset, a gloss effect moves across the refilled bars.

## Fixes

- Reset checks run 10 seconds after reset time. The previous delay was one minute.
- The pace chart omits the incorrect vertical line immediately after a reset.
- Account details always open at the top.
- Settings opens and closes faster. Rows appear without delay when scrolling immediately after opening Settings.
- Back returns to Resets when that tab opened the account.

## Good to know

Codex and Grok redemption follows the providers' apps. At this release, nobody had tested redemption with real accounts.
Use `adb logcat -s HeadroomResets` to inspect failures. Logs exclude tokens and account details.

## Installing

Download `headroom-1.1.0.apk` from this release and open it on your phone.
Headroom requires Android 16 or later. Use `headroom-1.1.0.apk.sha256` to check the download.
This release updates 1.0.0 and preserves your accounts.
