Headroom 1.1 shows the usage limit resets your accounts have, lets you use Codex and Grok resets,
and adds a Quick Settings tile.

## What is new

- **Usage limit resets.** Some providers give out resets that refill a limit early. Headroom now
  shows the ones each account has, and when each one expires, in the account's details and on the
  Resets tab.
  - Codex and Grok resets can be used from the app. A bottom sheet confirms which limits the reset
    refills, then refills the bars when it is done.
  - Claude's resets are shown for information only. Z.AI resets are not shown yet.
- **Quick Settings tile.** Add it from Settings. It opens Headroom, and shows either the next reset
  or the quota closest to its limit.
- **Expired sign-ins.** When an account's sign-in expires, its last numbers stay on screen, faded,
  with a button to sign in again. A notification says so once. Signing in again keeps the
  account's history, name and alerts.
- **Credits and new quotas.** Claude's one-time credits show how much is used and when they
  expire, in dollars when the provider says. A quota Headroom does not recognise yet still shows,
  with a note that says so. Every model-specific weekly limit Claude reports now shows too.
- **A rainbow sheen.** The refresh sheen and its sparkles now shimmer in soft rainbow colours, and
  a reset ends with a gloss sweeping over the refilled bars.

## Fixes

- Reset alerts now check 10 seconds after a reset instead of a minute, so they arrive sooner.
- The pace chart no longer starts with a vertical line right after a reset.
- An account's details always open at the top.
- Settings opens and closes faster, and its rows no longer pop in late when you scroll right away.
- From the Resets tab, back now returns to the Resets tab.

## Good to know

Using a Codex or Grok reset follows those providers' own apps, but it has not been tried with a
real account yet. If it fails, `adb logcat -s HeadroomResets` shows what happened, without tokens
or account details.

## Installing

Download `headroom-1.1.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. Headroom needs Android 16 or later.
You can check the download against `headroom-1.1.0.apk.sha256`. It installs over 1.0.0 and keeps
your accounts.
