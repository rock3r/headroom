Headroom 1.2 supports Z.AI reset redemption, expiry reminders and reset history.
Widgets also show each account's available reset count.

## What is new

- Headroom displays Z.AI's 5-hour and weekly resets. You can redeem them or ask Z.AI for new reset cards.
  Z.AI supplies resets through ZCode. Headroom asks for a separate browser sign-in when you first use this feature.
- Claude redemption requires "Redeem Claude resets (experimental)" in Settings under Resets.
  The setting defaults to off. Claude resets remain read-only until you enable it.
- Headroom sends a reminder about a day before an available reset expires.
  For example, a reminder says "Your ChatGPT Codex reset expires tomorrow at 06:18".
  Tapping opens that account's resets. Headroom sends at most one reminder daily.
  You can disable reminders in Settings under Resets.
- The new "Usage limit resets" card in Stats shows used and expired resets from the last year.
- Bars and Rings widgets show a small counter for usable resets on the home screen and lock screen.
- Long-pressing the Quick Settings tile opens its options in Headroom's Settings.

## Fixes

- Tapping the Quick Settings tile on a locked phone opens Headroom after unlocking.
  Previously, the app did not open.
- Settings and the overview open more smoothly.
- Restarting the app during Grok redemption no longer risks using two resets.
- Z.AI reset handling fixes several rare failures, including sign-out during ZCode credential storage.
- On wide screens, the sidebar refresh button aligns with the tabs.

## Good to know

Claude redemption remains experimental. Nobody has tested it with a real account.
Use `adb logcat -s HeadroomResets` to inspect failures. Logs exclude tokens and account details.

## Installing

Download `headroom-1.2.0.apk` from this release and open it on your phone.
Headroom requires Android 16 or later. Use `headroom-1.2.0.apk.sha256` to check the download.
This release updates 1.1.0 and preserves your accounts.
Obtainium can also install and update Headroom. The README contains its setup link.
