Headroom 1.2 lets you use Z.AI resets, reminds you before a reset expires, and keeps a history of
your resets. Widgets now show how many resets each account has.

## What is new

- **Z.AI resets.** Headroom now shows Z.AI's 5-hour and weekly resets, lets you use them, and
  lets you ask Z.AI for a new one. Z.AI serves resets from ZCode, so the first time you use one,
  Headroom asks you to sign in to ZCode in a browser tab.
- **Claude resets (experimental).** Headroom can now use Claude's resets too. Turn on "Redeem
  Claude resets (experimental)" in Settings, under Resets. It is off by default, and until you
  turn it on, Claude's resets stay read-only.
- **Reset reminders.** About a day before a reset you can use expires, Headroom sends a
  notification, for example "Your ChatGPT Codex reset expires tomorrow at 06:18". Tap it to open
  that account's resets. You get at most one reminder a day. You can turn reminders off in
  Settings, under Resets.
- **Reset history.** Headroom remembers the resets that were used or expired over the last year.
  The Stats tab shows them in a new "Usage limit resets" card.
- **Resets on widgets.** The Bars and Rings widgets, on the home screen and the lock screen, show a
  small counter when an account has resets it can use.
- **Quick Settings tile.** A long press on the tile now opens Headroom's Settings at the tile's
  options.

## Fixes

- A tap on the Quick Settings tile while the phone is locked now opens Headroom after you unlock.
  Before, Headroom did not open.
- Settings and the overview open more smoothly.
- A Grok reset could be used twice if the app restarted while it was being used. This no longer
  happens.
- Several rare problems with Z.AI resets are fixed, for example when you sign out while the app is
  saving a ZCode sign-in.
- On wide screens, the refresh button on the side bar lines up with the tabs.

## Good to know

Using a Claude reset is experimental, and it has not been tried with a real account yet. If it
fails, `adb logcat -s HeadroomResets` shows what happened, without tokens or account details.

## Installing

Download `headroom-1.2.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. Headroom needs Android 16 or later.
You can check the download against `headroom-1.2.0.apk.sha256`. It installs over 1.1.0 and keeps
your accounts. You can also install and update Headroom with Obtainium: see the README.
