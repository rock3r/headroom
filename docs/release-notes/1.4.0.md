Headroom 1.4 shows a Grok account's Cursor usage beside its own limits.
Sign-ins that finish in the browser now come back to the app by themselves, and ZCode's sign-in opens at once and hands you straight to the resets.

## What is new

- A Grok account that also uses Cursor shows what Cursor reports: Cursor Models, Other Models and the Grok Bot pool, next to the account's Grok limits.
  Cursor serves those pools from its own backend, which does not accept the account's xAI sign-in, so Headroom asks for a separate browser sign-in to Cursor the first time.
  The prompt appears on the account's card and on its detail screen. "Not now" hides the card's nudge and leaves the account's own screen as the way back.
  A Cursor read that fails never fails the Grok numbers: the account says the read failed, and its Cursor pools come back on the next sync.

## Fixes

- Signing in to ZCode or Cursor in the browser returns you to Headroom and closes the tab. Earlier versions could leave the tab open with the app waiting behind it: the tab was opened into the browser's own task, and Android froze the app while you were away, which stopped Headroom from noticing that the sign-in had finished.
  Headroom now opens the page in its own task and keeps a quiet notification for as long as a sign-in waits.
- ZCode's sign-in from the reset sheet opens the page at once, instead of waiting for the round trip that makes it, because the page is readied while you read the sheet.
  The sheet also stays open after you sign in and shows the account's resets, rather than closing on you.
- A Cursor read that failed is read again, instead of asking you to sign in a second time and replacing a credential that still works.
  A Cursor sign-in the provider refuses asks you to sign in again, because retrying cannot mend it.
- A sign-in that fails says why. A refused or interrupted Cursor sign-in used to look exactly like one you had never tried.
- Cursor's pools never take over an account's headline, pace or overview sorting. They sit beside the account's own limits, which is what the card describes, even when Cursor's weekly pool is the longer-lived one.
- The account's own screen no longer offers the Cursor connection while its Grok sign-in has expired: connecting could not have been saved in that state.
- A reset used just before it expired is no longer counted twice in Stats. Headroom records a redemption at the time of the first attempt, so the reset it belongs to settles even when the answer to that attempt only arrives after the reset has expired.

## Installing

Download `headroom-1.4.0.apk` from this release and open it on your phone.
Headroom requires Android 16 or later. Use `headroom-1.4.0.apk.sha256` to check the download.
This release updates 1.3.0 and preserves your accounts, their usage history and their reset records.
Obtainium can also install and update Headroom. The README contains its setup link.
