Headroom 1.5 moves to a new phone with you: your accounts and settings come back after you restore from a backup, without signing in again.
JetBrains accounts with more than one license now lead with the credits of all of them, and the app draws far fewer frames when nothing moves.

## What is new

- Android backup now carries Headroom over to a new phone: accounts, settings, alerts, widgets, usage history and reset records.
  Sign-in tokens stay encrypted in the backup. The key that unlocks them travels separately, end-to-end encrypted, as an Android restore credential, so the backup alone cannot read them.
  After a restore, Headroom unlocks the accounts and starts syncing, and reschedules its reset alerts and reminders. Signing out of the last account removes the restore credential.
- A JetBrains account with more than one license or workspace seat leads with a Combined window that pools the credits of every license and seat.
  It resets when the soonest license does, and its pace weighs each license by its credits, so one small license that ran out no longer makes the whole account read 100%.
  The per-license rows stay below it. The Combined window's usage history starts with this release.

## Fixes

- Connecting Cursor or ZCode to an account checks whose sign-in it is. A Cursor or ZCode account that is already connected to another of your accounts, or that differs from the one this account had, is refused with a message that says so, instead of quietly showing someone else's usage.
- The empty "Signing in" notification channel that 1.4.0 left in your notification settings is removed.
- Smoother and lighter on high refresh rate displays:
  - The refresh shimmer does much less GPU work per frame, so a refresh no longer stutters.
  - The wavy quota bars, the next reset card's shape and the detail's ring move at 60 fps instead of on every display frame, at 90 Hz, 120 Hz and 144 Hz alike. An overview at rest redraws about half as often.
  - Headroom ships a baseline profile for its own code, so it starts and opens screens faster right after an install or update.

## Installing

Download `headroom-1.5.0.apk` from this release and open it on your phone.
Headroom requires Android 16 or later. Use `headroom-1.5.0.apk.sha256` to check the download.
This release updates 1.4.1 and preserves your accounts, their usage history and their reset records.
Obtainium can also install and update Headroom. The README contains its setup link.
