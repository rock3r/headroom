Headroom 1.3 keeps a year of usage history and shows it in a new chart you can pan and zoom.
It also adds Muse, redeems Claude resets out of the box and explains expired API keys.
Headroom 1.2.0 was never published, so this release also brings everything 1.2.0 added.

## What is new

- Account details show a usage history chart. It overlays the account's weekly and monthly limits, with a chip for each one to show or hide it.
  A dashed line in each limit's colour marks every reset. The even-pace line and the projection show in the current period.
  Drag to move through time, pinch to zoom, and press and hold to read the values at a moment.
  The Period, 1M, 3M and 1Y buttons jump to a range. TalkBack describes the range on screen and offers scroll and zoom actions.
- Headroom keeps a year of usage. Older usage is kept in less detail: every sync for a week, hourly for a month, every 3 hours for three months and every 12 hours for the rest of the year.
  You can turn this off in Settings under Usage history. Headroom then keeps the last 60 days, as before.
- Headroom supports Muse. Sign in with a device code to see its 5-hour and weekly limits.
- Headroom redeems Claude resets without a setting. You can still turn this off in Settings under Resets.
- When an API key expires, Headroom says so and offers "Replace API key". Earlier versions asked you to sign in again, which does not renew a key.
  Headroom checks a new key before saving it. A key the provider rejects is not saved, and the screen says why.

## Also new since 1.1 (from 1.2.0)

- Headroom displays Z.AI's 5-hour and weekly resets. You can redeem them or ask Z.AI for new reset cards.
  Z.AI supplies resets through ZCode. Headroom asks for a separate browser sign-in when you first use this feature.
- Headroom sends a reminder about a day before an available reset expires.
  Tapping opens that account's resets. Headroom sends at most one reminder daily. You can disable reminders in Settings under Resets.
- The "Usage limit resets" card in Stats shows used and expired resets from the last year.
- Bars and Rings widgets show a small counter for usable resets on the home screen and lock screen.
- Long-pressing the Quick Settings tile opens its options in Headroom's Settings.

## Fixes

- Stats stay readable with a year of history. The reset card shows your latest resets in two rows, and the heatmap uses the last month.
- Tapping the Quick Settings tile on a locked phone opens Headroom after unlocking.
- Settings and the overview open more smoothly.
- Restarting the app during Grok redemption no longer risks using two resets.
- Z.AI reset handling fixes several rare failures, including sign-out during ZCode credential storage.
- On wide screens, the sidebar refresh button aligns with the tabs.

## Installing

Download `headroom-1.3.0.apk` from this release and open it on your phone.
Headroom requires Android 16 or later. Use `headroom-1.3.0.apk.sha256` to check the download.
This release updates 1.1.0 and preserves your accounts and their history.
Obtainium can also install and update Headroom. The README contains its setup link.
